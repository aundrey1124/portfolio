How I work

From questions to trusted decisions

I own the entire BI delivery lifecycle rather than treating charts as the final step.

01 · Discover & define

Interview stakeholders, map their decisions, document KPIs and business rules, and validate the reporting sources.

02 · Design for clarity

Prototype in Figma, prioritize the most actionable metrics, and create intuitive filtering, drilldowns, and accessible layouts.

03 · Build & validate

Develop semantic metrics and optimized SQL, embed dashboards in React apps, secure access, and test calculations against source data.

04 · Deliver & improve

Launch with QA and documentation, gather user feedback, and tune loading speed, reliability, and adoption.
